# P4 — Feature Ablation / Feature-Family Comparison

> **All P4 model-selection decisions were made without outer-test data, under protocol v1.0 frozen at
> `p2-protocol-freeze`, on a branch started at `p3-loso-baseline` (`950a514`).**
> - Each family's TCN configuration and final epoch count came from inner validation on the two training subjects
>   only.
> - All 15 family × fold selections were written to `configs/experiments/v1.0/p4_selected_configs.yaml` and committed
>   (`8cac443`) before any P4 final model was trained or any held-out subject was evaluated with a P4 model.
> - Each P4 final model was evaluated on its held-out subject exactly once.
> - RAW was not re-run. The RAW TCN and the training-mean predictor are the P3 results frozen at `p3-loso-baseline`.

## 1. Purpose

- RQ3 asks what movement-derived and contact-structure features contribute to strict leave-one-subject-out
  estimation of temperature and humidity.
- P4 measures how the declared feature families change the P3 strict-LOSO error under the unchanged protocol v1.0.
- It is **not** an attempt to improve P3. The P3 result (RAW TCN worse than the training-mean predictor for temperature
  in 3/3 subjects; better for humidity in 2/3) is frozen and is the reference.
- The claim scope is P3's: an unseen subject under the observed combined subject–period–season–device shift, with
  n = 3 held-out subjects.

## 2. Frozen Protocol and P3 Reference

- **Unchanged from P3:**
  - folds 1/2/3 hold out User01, User02 (both mats) and User07;
  - two-way subject-level inner splits;
  - 40 s windows of 8 × 5 s bins, stride 20 s; the last row of each bin; a gap > 5 s ends a segment;
  - P / 4095 with 4095 kept; targets z-scored on the training partition of each fit; labels only where both targets
    are valid;
  - the 16-configuration grid, AdamW, weight decay 1e-4, batch 256, at most 50 epochs, patience 5;
  - selection seed 0; half-up rounded final epochs (D-043); final seeds 0/1/2;
  - D-041 metrics.
- **Reference rows**, read from the committed P3 files (their SHA-256 is recorded in
  `outputs/metrics/p4/p4_reference_provenance.csv`):
  - the RAW TCN per seed (`paper/tables/p3_tcn_outer_by_seed.csv`);
  - the training-mean predictor (`p3_training_mean_by_fold.csv`);
  - the strata (`p3_secondary_strata.csv`) and the selections (`p3_selected_configs.csv`).
  The code refuses to aggregate if any of these files differs from `p3-loso-baseline`. Before use, it checks that the
  per-seed table reproduces the committed P3 summary (to 1e-12).
- **Search budget:** P3's 96 RAW inner runs plus P4's 480 give the 576 runs fixed in D-040.

## 3. Feature Families

| Family | Inputs per step | Definition (D-039; `src/features/pressure_features.py`) |
|---|---|---|
| RAW | 6 | p_c / 4095 (P3 reference, not re-run) |
| MOVEMENT | 10 | Δp_c / 4095 (6); mean \|Δp_c\| / 4095; Δ(Σp / (6·4095)); Δ(active channels / 6); dominant-channel switch; step 0 = 0 |
| CONTACT | 11 | Σp / (6·4095); active channels / 6; shares p_c / Σp (6); normalised share entropy; maximum share; across-channel SD / 4095; an all-zero frame gives zero shares, entropy and maximum share |
| RAW+MOVEMENT | 16 | RAW then MOVEMENT columns |
| RAW+CONTACT | 17 | RAW then CONTACT columns |
| RAW+MOVEMENT+CONTACT | 27 | RAW, MOVEMENT, CONTACT columns |

- All features are geometry-free. No channel coordinate, centre of pressure or left/right or head/foot reading is
  used (OPEN-20). There is no feature selection.
- **Guard.** Before each run the code checks the family's composition, order, width and names against
  `protocol.yaml` and D-039. The leakage gate receives the family's exact input list; MOVEMENT and CONTACT runs contain
  no RAW input.
- **Tests.** The formulas are pinned by `tests/test_p4_features.py`:
  - MOVEMENT step-0 padding, channel deltas, mean |Δ|, sum and active-count deltas, and dominant switches including
    ties and empty frames;
  - CONTACT sum, active count, shares summing to 1, the zero frame, entropy bounds, maximum share and SD;
  - column order, the dimensions 10/11/16/17/27, the absence of target, identity, calendar, phase and event inputs,
    and chunked = whole-array computation.
- **What the comparison is.** Every representation family gets the identical frozen outer split and the same
  pre-declared model-selection budget; each family selects its own TCN configuration. It is therefore not a
  strict architectural ablation that removes one feature from an otherwise fixed model.

## 4. Execution / Search Budget

- **Order enforced by code:**
  1. inner search: 5 families × 3 folds × 16 configurations × 2 inner splits = **480 runs**, seed 0;
  2. per-fold selection frozen for 15 family × fold combinations, then the committed selection file (`8cac443`);
  3. final runs: 5 families × 3 folds × seeds 0/1/2 = **45 runs**, each evaluated once on its held-out subject;
  4. aggregation against the frozen P3 reference.
  `run_final` refuses to start unless the selection file is committed and unmodified, and records its commit.
- **Early stopping** (all 480 inner runs):
  - runs ended after 6–37 epochs (median 9); no run reached the 50-epoch cap;
  - the best inner-validation epoch was 1–32 (median 4).
  - MOVEMENT trained longest (median 10 epochs run, best epoch up to 32).
- **Compute:** 10.8 GPU-hours for the inner search (inflated by the contention below) and 48 min for the 45 final runs.
- **Interrupted runs:**
  - For part of the inner search, another project's process held about 12 of the GPU's 16 GB. This oversubscribed GPU
    memory and slowed P4 epochs by 10–100×.
  - To relieve it, two of P4's own runner processes were stopped. This interrupted two running inner runs
    (`movement/fold3_A_cfg00`, `movement/fold2_B_cfg00`), neither of which had completed.
  - Each was re-run from scratch as attempt 2 after the other process ended. Attempt 1 is kept in its `status.json`
    history.
  - No completed run was repeated, and nothing else was re-run.
- **Provenance:**
  - all 480 inner runs record commit `39a89e0` (the pipeline commit) with a clean tracked tree;
  - all 45 final runs record `8cac443` with a clean tree;
  - every run records deterministic mode switched on (the P3 §14 note is resolved).

## 7. Movement Contribution

**A = RAW+MOVEMENT − RAW** (Table P4-6):
- **Temperature:**
  - MAE falls by 0.497 / 0.073 / 0.059 °C (User01 / User02 / User07): it improved in all three held-out subjects, in
    MAE and in RMSE.
  - The unweighted mean falls by 0.210 °C (−6.3 %).
  - 8 of 9 seed-matched pairs improve. The one exception is User02 (+0.007).
  - The size of the effect is uneven: User01 carries most of it; the User02 and User07 changes are < 0.08 °C.
- **Humidity:** there is no consistent contribution.
  - MAE Δ +0.333 / −0.162 / +0.108 (1/3 improved); RMSE unweighted Δ −0.001.
  - 3 of 9 seed-matched pairs improve.
- **What changed** (Table P4-11): the User01 temperature gain comes mostly from a smaller error SD (−0.715 °C), not from
  a smaller offset (|bias| −0.106 °C).
- **Reading:** temporal pressure dynamics add some predictive information for temperature under this protocol, mostly
  by reducing window-to-window error for User01. This is an association, not a causal statement.

## 8. Contact Contribution

**B = RAW+CONTACT − RAW** (Table P4-6):
- **Humidity:**
  - MAE falls by 1.294 / 0.352 / 0.002 %RH, and RMSE falls for all three subjects too.
  - This counts as improved in all three held-out subjects, but the User07 change (−0.002 MAE, −0.017 RMSE) is
    negligible.
  - The unweighted mean falls by 0.549 %RH (−3.0 %). 7 of 9 seed-matched pairs improve.
- **Temperature:** no gain.
  - MAE Δ +0.118 / −0.051 / +0.035 (1/3 improved); unweighted mean +0.034 °C.
  - 3 of 9 seed-matched pairs improve.
- **What changed:** for User01 humidity, the offset shrinks (|bias| 19.043 → 17.119 %RH) while the error SD grows
  (+0.751). Part of the gain is therefore a smaller level offset.
- **Scale dependence:**
  - CONTACT shares and entropy do not depend on the absolute pressure scale. They were not assumed to be domain
    invariant, and the results do not show it.
  - On User01's old sensor (`s1`), RAW+CONTACT has a larger temperature error than RAW (3.707 vs 3.477 °C); CONTACT
    alone is lower (3.049).
  - Any reduced dependence on the absolute scale is visible only for CONTACT without RAW on `s1`. With the RAW
    channels present, it disappears.

## 17. Interpretation for RQ3

1. **Does adding MOVEMENT to RAW lower cross-subject error?**
   - For temperature, yes, in all three held-out subjects (−0.210 °C unweighted MAE; 8/9 seed-matched pairs), mostly
     through User01.
   - For humidity, no consistent change.
2. **Does adding CONTACT to RAW lower it?**
   - For humidity, yes, in all three subjects, with a negligible User07 part (−0.549 %RH unweighted; 7/9 pairs).
   - For temperature, no.
3. **How much information do the standalone representations carry?**
   - MOVEMENT alone gives the lowest temperature error of the six families but degrades humidity. Its temperature
     behaviour is closer to the training-mean predictor.
   - CONTACT alone is about as informative as RAW for both targets.
4. **Is RAW+MOVEMENT+CONTACT the strongest?** No. It is rank 4 for temperature and rank 2 for humidity (MAE), and the
   movement and contact gains do not add up.
5. **Are family effects consistent across subjects?**
   - Direction: 3/3 for movement on temperature and contact on humidity.
   - Size: dominated by User01. User02 and User07 changes are mostly below 0.15 °C and 0.51 %RH.
6. **Do feature families reduce the domain-level offset?**
   - Not for User02: in every family the error is ≈ 100 % offset.
   - Partly for User01 humidity with contact features.
   - Movement lowers variation, not offset.
7. **Is any family better than the training-mean predictor for temperature?**
   - Not on the unweighted mean.
   - For one subject each (MOVEMENT–User07, RAW+MOVEMENT–User01), in MAE and RMSE but not with all seeds.
8. **Is the P3 humidity gain more stable with movement or contact?**
   - With contact, it is larger for User01 and User02 and less seed-dependent for User01.
   - The User07 failure and the 2/3 pattern are unchanged. Movement does not stabilise it.

**Overall:** under the frozen strict protocol, pressure-derived representations lower a single subject's error by at
most about 0.5 °C (A, User01) and 1.3 %RH (B, User01). Movement dynamics help temperature and contact structure helps
humidity, mostly for User01. None of them removes the subject–period–season–device level offset that dominates strict
LOSO error, and none makes the TCN beat the training-mean predictor for temperature. These effects are not large
against between-subject differences, and they would not justify changing the protocol or the P5 primary family.

## 18. Limitations

- **n = 3 held-out subjects.** Subject, season, period, device and microclimate are confounded in every fold (A11, P1).
  x/3 counts are descriptive, and there is no population-level or significance claim.
- **Family comparisons include each family's own selected configuration and epoch count.** They describe
  representations under an equal selection budget, not the removal of one feature from a fixed model.
- **Selection has a single seed and a flat inner landscape**, as in P3. Effects of about 0.1 °C / 0.5 %RH are of the
  order of seed spread for User01.
- **Many comparisons are made:** 7 comparisons × 2 targets × 2 metrics, with 3 subjects each. Some mixed-sign patterns
  are expected by chance. Only effects that agree across subjects and most seeds are emphasised.
- **Seed-index pairing is a bookkeeping pairing.** The night-level paired bootstrap and heater-context strata are
  deferred to P6.
- **The error-SD split is descriptive** and is derived from the declared RMSE and bias.
- **The RAW reference comes from P3's runs.** The P4 code path for RAW is bitwise identical on synthetic data
  (`test_raw_through_the_p4_runner_equals_the_p3_runner`), but RAW was not re-run on study data. By design, its
  selection and seeds are P3's.
- **GPU contention** during part of the inner search changed the run time only. Determinism was checked by the clean
  reproduction (§19).
