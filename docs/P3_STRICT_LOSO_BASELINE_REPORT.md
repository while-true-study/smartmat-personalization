## 1. Purpose

Measure how accurately a model trained on two primary subjects estimates temperature and humidity for the third,
never-seen subject, under the frozen protocol. Each held-out fold also differs in collection period, season,
sensor/device configuration and microclimate (P1, D-029). The result therefore describes **an unseen subject under
the observed combined domain shift**. It is not population-level generalisation and not a pure subject effect.

## 2. Frozen protocol reference

- **Folds** (committed `outer_folds.csv`):
  - 1 = User01 held out;
  - 2 = User02 held out (22480 + 22482 together);
  - 3 = User07 held out.
- **Inner splits** (`inner_folds.csv`): two-way, subject-level.
- **Windows:** 40 s, 8 × 5-s bins, stride 20 s.
  - Each step is the last observed row in its bin; the maximum gap is 5 s.
  - Windows are built inside the partitions from the split files.
  - A window is labelled only if both targets are valid at its last step.
- **Inputs:** RAW only, P / 4095, with 4095 kept. Targets are z-scored with training-partition statistics; metrics
  are in °C and %RH.
- **Models:**
  - training-mean predictor (outer training pool);
  - RAW TCN from D-040 (16-configuration grid, AdamW, weight decay 1e-4, batch 256, at most 50 epochs, patience 5).
- **Selection:** inner criterion (MAE_T/sd_T + MAE_H/sd_H)/2, averaged over inner A and B, with seed 0. The final
  epoch count is the half-up rounded mean of the two inner best epochs (D-043).
- **Final models:** whole outer training pool, seeds 0, 1 and 2.
- **Primary endpoints:** MAE and RMSE per target, per held-out subject, and the unweighted mean over the three
  subjects.
- P3 runs neither MOVEMENT/CONTACT families (P4), nor personalization (P5), nor sensitivity windows (P6).

## 3. Execution and reproducibility

- **Order enforced by code:**
  1. training-mean;
  2. inner search (3 folds × 16 configurations × 2 inner splits = 96 runs);
  3. per-fold selection frozen, then the committed selection file;
  4. final runs (3 folds × 3 seeds = 9), each evaluated once on its held-out subject;
  5. aggregation.
  `run_final` refuses to start without the committed selection file.
- **Every run:**
  - passes the frozen P2 leakage gate first (fail closed);
  - stores `leakage_check.json`, `run_meta.json` (commit, dirty flag, P2 tag, protocol, split and canonical
    hashes, seed, environment), `config.yaml`, history, checkpoint and metrics, plus predictions for outer
    evaluations;
  - stores `status.json` with artifact SHA-256. A run is complete only if every artifact still matches.
- **Environment** (D-043): PyTorch 2.12.0 + CUDA 12.6 on one NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Ti, with deterministic
  algorithms and cuDNN deterministic mode. A synthetic smoke test reproduced predictions bitwise.
- **Reproduction criterion, fixed before any final run:**
  - the 9 final runs are re-run from a clean checkout using only the committed code, protocol and selection file;
  - every final-run MAE and RMSE (per target) must agree within **1e-6** (°C / %RH);
  - every training-mean metric must agree within **1e-9**.

## 4. Training-mean baseline

The predictor is the per-target mean of the labelled windows of the outer training pool, applied to every labelled
window of the held-out subject (Table P3-1, `paper/tables/p3_training_mean_by_fold.csv`).

| Held out | Training pool (windows) | Test windows | MAE T (°C) | MAE H (%RH) | Bias T | Bias H |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| User01 | User02 + User07 (284,970) | 289,437 | 2.735 | 23.058 | +2.637 | +22.232 |
| User02 | User01 + User07 (430,408) | 143,999 | 4.592 | 27.629 | −4.592 | −27.495 |
| User07 | User01 + User02 (433,436) | 140,971 | 1.815 | 7.994 | +1.098 | −2.122 |

Nearly the whole error is a level offset between the held-out domain and the training pool:
- |bias| / MAE = 0.96 (T) and 0.96 (H) for User01;
- 1.00 and 1.00 for User02;
- 0.60 and 0.27 for User07.

## 5. Nested RAW-TCN selection

- All 96 inner runs completed: 3 folds × 16 configurations × inner splits A and B, seed 0, 88 min of training in
  total.
- **Early stopping** ended every run after 6–23 epochs (median 8). The best inner-validation epoch was 1–18
  (median 3). On the other training subject, the validation criterion is best after very few epochs.
- **Score spread** over the 16 configurations was narrow (Table P3-6):
  - fold 1: 1.755–1.852;
  - fold 2: 1.139–1.170;
  - fold 3: 2.491–2.597.
  The declared hyperparameters change the cross-subject criterion far less than the fold does.
- One inner run (`fold2_A_cfg00`) has two attempts. The first process was killed by the operating system for low
  memory while it was loading data, before any training: another application held about 30 GB. The attempt is kept in
  its `status.json` history. Commit `257eb6b` lowered P3's memory without changing any window, which was verified on
  real data. No completed run was repeated.

## 8. Fold / subject heterogeneity

- **Errors differ more than two-fold between held-out subjects.**
  - TCN temperature MAE: 2.045 (User07) to 4.822 (User02).
  - Humidity MAE: 9.972 (User07) to 24.591 (User02).
- **The error is mostly an offset** (Table P3-2).
  - User02 is under-predicted everywhere: TCN bias −4.822 °C = −MAE, and −24.173 %RH.
  - User01 humidity is over-predicted (+19.043 %RH).
  - This matches the P1 level differences between the domains: User02 is 29–32 °C and 52–67 %RH; User01 is 25–26 °C
    and 25–28 %RH.
  - Each training pool's mean level lies on the other side of the held-out subject's level (outer scaler means:
    28.4 °C / 48.8 %RH in fold 1, 26.0 °C / 30.7 %RH in fold 2).
- **Per-night MAE** (Table P3-8) varies widely within subjects: for example, User02 humidity has an IQR of
  16.7–32.1 %RH across 51 nights. The subject-level numbers therefore average heterogeneous nights.

## 9. Device and phase diagnostics

they were seen.

## 11. Comparison with the training-mean baseline

Differences are TCN minus training-mean, as seed means; negative = TCN better.

| Held out | ΔMAE temperature (°C) | ΔMAE humidity (%RH) | Seeds on the same side |
|---|---|---|---|
| User01 | +0.390 | −2.773 | 3/3 and 3/3 |
| User02 | +0.231 | −3.038 | 3/3 and 3/3 |
| User07 | +0.230 | +1.978 | 3/3 and 3/3 |
| Unweighted mean | +0.284 | −1.277 | — |

- **Temperature:** the RAW TCN is worse than the training-mean predictor for every held-out subject, in MAE and in
  RMSE.
- **Humidity:** it is better for User01 and User02 (MAE −12 % and −11 %; RMSE also lower) and worse for User07
  (MAE +25 %, RMSE +28 %).
- The unweighted-mean humidity improvement (18.28 vs 19.56 %RH, −6.5 %) is smaller than the between-subject
  spread. With n = 3 it is not a population-level result. The RMSE means differ by only 0.28 %RH.

## 12. Interpretation for RQ1

- **Q1 — Is the RAW TCN better than training-mean?**
  - Temperature: no, for any held-out subject.
  - Humidity: yes for 2 of 3 subjects (User01, User02), no for User07.
  The neural baseline does not dominate the level baseline under strict LOSO.
- **Q2 — Which target is harder?**
  - In units of the outer-training-pool SD, the errors are similar for both targets:
    - TCN temperature MAE / SD = 1.09, 2.65, 0.71 (User01, User02, User07);
    - humidity = 1.31, 2.07, 0.52.
  - Temperature is the harder one to improve on: 0/3 subjects beat the baseline, against 2/3 for humidity.
  - Both are dominated by level offsets between the held-out domain and the training pool.
- **Q3 — Fold differences:** large, more than two-fold in MAE. User02 (the warmest, most humid domain, recorded
  July–September on two mats) is the hardest; User07 is the easiest.
- **Q4 — User01 s1/s2:** the TCN's temperature error differs between phases (3.48 vs 2.74 °C) in the opposite
  direction to the baseline. This is consistent with the old sensor's larger pressure scale (P1) affecting the TCN.
  It is a diagnostic only.
- **Q5 — User02 22480/22482:** large differences (3.24 vs 5.88 °C; 15.0 vs 30.9 %RH), explained mostly by the two
  mats' different microclimates (bias ≈ −MAE).
- **Q6 — 22482 normal vs response shift:** no larger error in the shift phase. The differences track the target level,
  not the P1 channel fault.
- **Q7 — Seeds:** seed variability is much smaller than fold/domain differences and never reverses a TCN–baseline
  comparison.

**Overall:** under the frozen strict protocol, pressure-based estimation of an unseen subject's temperature and
humidity is dominated by the combined subject–period–season–device level shift. A RAW-pressure TCN recovers part of
the humidity offset for two held-out subjects, but it does not improve on predicting the training mean for
temperature. This is the result, not a reason to change the protocol.

## 13. Limitations

- **n = 3** held-out subjects: no population inference and no significance claim. Subject, season, period, device
  and microclimate are confounded in every fold (A11, P1).
- **The RAW TCN is a strict-LOSO neural baseline, not a complete replication** of the prior method. Movement fusion is
  compared in P4. Prior-study numbers were not used for any P3 choice.
- **Model selection used a single seed** and two-subject inner validation. The flat inner score landscape means the
  selection carries little information.
- **Night-level uncertainty** (cluster bootstrap) and the heater-context strata are P6. The window-weighted and
  per-night results here are descriptive.
- **Heater-controlled microclimate:** targets are temperature/humidity of a controlled mat microclimate (OPEN-10,
  D-038). Errors include offsets in that control regime.
