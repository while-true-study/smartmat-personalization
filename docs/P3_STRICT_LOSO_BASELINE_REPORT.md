## 1. Purpose

Measure how accurately a model trained on two primary subjects estimates temperature and humidity for the third,
never-seen subject, under the frozen protocol. Each held-out fold also differs in collection period, season,
sensor/device configuration and microclimate (P1, D-029). The result therefore describes **an unseen subject under
the observed combined domain shift**. It is not population-level generalisation and not a pure subject effect.

## 2. Frozen protocol reference

- **Folds** (committed `outer_folds.csv`):
  - 1 = User01 held out;
  - 2 = User02 held out (22480 + 22482 together);
  - 3 = User07 held out.
- **Inner splits** (`inner_folds.csv`): two-way, subject-level.
- **Windows:** 40 s, 8 × 5-s bins, stride 20 s.
  - Each step is the last observed row in its bin; the maximum gap is 5 s.
  - Windows are built inside the partitions from the split files.
  - A window is labelled only if both targets are valid at its last step.
- **Inputs:** RAW only, P / 4095, with 4095 kept. Targets are z-scored with training-partition statistics; metrics
  are in °C and %RH.
- **Models:**
  - training-mean predictor (outer training pool);
  - RAW TCN from D-040 (16-configuration grid, AdamW, weight decay 1e-4, batch 256, at most 50 epochs, patience 5).
- **Selection:** inner criterion (MAE_T/sd_T + MAE_H/sd_H)/2, averaged over inner A and B, with seed 0. The final
  epoch count is the half-up rounded mean of the two inner best epochs (D-043).
- **Final models:** whole outer training pool, seeds 0, 1 and 2.
- **Primary endpoints:** MAE and RMSE per target, per held-out subject, and the unweighted mean over the three
  subjects.
- P3 runs neither MOVEMENT/CONTACT families (P4), nor personalization (P5), nor sensitivity windows (P6).

## 3. Execution and reproducibility

- **Order enforced by code:**
  1. training-mean;
  2. inner search (3 folds × 16 configurations × 2 inner splits = 96 runs);
  3. per-fold selection frozen, then the committed selection file;
  4. final runs (3 folds × 3 seeds = 9), each evaluated once on its held-out subject;
  5. aggregation.
  `run_final` refuses to start without the committed selection file.
- **Every run:**
  - passes the frozen P2 leakage gate first (fail closed);
  - stores `leakage_check.json`, `run_meta.json` (commit, dirty flag, P2 tag, protocol, split and canonical
    hashes, seed, environment), `config.yaml`, history, checkpoint and metrics, plus predictions for outer
    evaluations;
  - stores `status.json` with artifact SHA-256. A run is complete only if every artifact still matches.
- **Environment** (D-043): PyTorch 2.12.0 + CUDA 12.6 on one NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Ti, with deterministic
  algorithms and cuDNN deterministic mode. A synthetic smoke test reproduced predictions bitwise.
- **Reproduction criterion, fixed before any final run:**
  - the 9 final runs are re-run from a clean checkout using only the committed code, protocol and selection file;
  - every final-run MAE and RMSE (per target) must agree within **1e-6** (°C / %RH);
  - every training-mean metric must agree within **1e-9**.
