## 1. Design (summary)

- **Data:** User03 rows reconstructed from the valid CSV export and the second-level timestamps of the paired TXT
  export. Only value-identical minutes are kept, and the canonical_v1 rules and the v1.0 window rule are applied.
- **Models:** the three frozen P3 RAW-TCN configurations, trained on all labelled windows of User01, User02 and
  User07, each with its frozen epoch count, seeds 0–2.
- **Comparator:** the source training mean.
- **User03's role:** it is used only for evaluation.
- **Metrics:** the v1.2 definitions (population SDs).

## 3. Reading (under the pre-registered rules)

- **Constant baseline versus network.** For both targets the training-mean predictor had the lower MAE than every one
  of the three source-only RAW-TCN configurations, on almost every night (Tables P9-1 and P9-3). Under plan §9 the
  training mean is **better** for both targets. No night-bootstrap interval was computed (fewer than 10 nights); the
  seed ranges of the network's MAE lie entirely above the training-mean MAE.
- **Level offset.** Both predictors under-estimate User03's temperature and humidity (negative bias). The network's
  offset is larger for humidity, so the unseen-domain level offset of the primary analysis recurs in this
  additional subject.
- **Variation without co-variation.** The network's predictions vary (Q well above zero; for humidity they vary about
  twice as much as the target), but the pooled and within-night correlations are near zero.
  - For humidity, the within-night correlations are small and positive in every configuration, with positive
    per-night correlations on most nights. They stay below the pre-registered 0.10 floor, so within-night
    co-variation is classified **absent** (plan §9).
  - For temperature, the correlations are near zero and change sign between nights.
- **Residual variation.** R exceeds 1 for both targets: the network adds variation that does not follow the target,
  as the base models did under strict LOSO (v1.1).
- **Relation to the N = 3 findings: strengthened** for both targets. In one additional held-out subject, pressure-based
  estimation under the tested formulation (40-s RAW windows, TCN, the frozen configurations and schedules) again
  showed no advantage over a simple level baseline and no demonstrable within-night co-variation.

## 4. Limitations

- **Post hoc, one subject, seven nights.** P9 is post hoc. It adds one subject with seven nights, so it gives no
  population inference, no night-bootstrap interval and no replication claim. User03 is not a primary subject.
- **Reconstructed provenance.** The reconstruction depends on the TXT export's timestamps. The data provider has
  confirmed that the setting problem behind the D-017 exclusion does not affect them (D-062), so the results are no
  longer conditional; the values themselves are verified identical to the valid CSV export. The confirmation covers
  the timestamps of these seven nights only. The rest of `user06_auxiliary` stays excluded, and the reconstruction
  is still a two-source reconstruction rather than a single validated export.
- **Model choice.** The source-only models use the frozen P3 configurations and epoch counts, which were selected on
  two-subject training pools, not on all three primary subjects. No configuration was chosen on User03.
- **No personalization.** RQ2 personalization was not evaluated on User03.

## 5. Reproduction

`scripts/verify_p9_user03_reproduction.py` rebuilds the external artifact in memory, compares it with the committed
manifest, retrains the nine models into a separate root and compares the User03 predictions bitwise. Its result is
recorded in `outputs/metrics/p9_user03/p9_user03_reproduction_check.json`.

**Final closure rerun (D-063, 2026-09-16).** The prediction writer was changed from `np.save` to
`np.lib.format.write_array` (the same `.npy` payload, written through the repository's write guard) after the
previous full rerun, so the nine models were retrained from scratch on the frozen protocol and compared again:
