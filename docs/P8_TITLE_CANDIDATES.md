# P8 — Title candidates

> No title is selected yet (PI decision). Candidates are ranked against four criteria:
> 1. both halves of the story: unseen-domain generalization **and** chronological personalization with negative
>    transfer;
> 2. no population-level overstatement;
> 3. Applied Sciences style (descriptive, searchable terms);
> 4. length (≈ 20 words or fewer).

| Rank | Candidate | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **When Does User Adaptation Help? Chronological Personalization of Smart-Mat Temperature and Humidity Estimation under Unseen-User Domain Shift** | States the central question, including that adaptation can fail. Keeps "unseen-user domain shift" and "chronological personalization". | Long (17 words). A question-form title may need editor approval. |
| 2 | **Chronological Personalization for Smart-Mat Temperature and Humidity Estimation under Unseen-User Domain Shift: Offset Correction and Negative Transfer** | Names both outcomes explicitly. Searchable. | Long (17 words). The subtitle carries the key finding. |
| 3 | **Limited-Data User Adaptation for Smart-Mat Environmental Estimation: Benefits and Negative Transfer under Temporal Drift** | Conservative ("limited-data"). "Negative transfer" and "temporal drift" are prominent. | "Environmental estimation" is vaguer than temperature/humidity. Cross-subject evaluation is only implicit. |
| 4 | **When User Adaptation Helps or Hurts: Chronological Personalization of Smart-Mat Temperature and Humidity Estimation** | Short and memorable. The two-sided finding is up front. | Drops the unseen-user / cross-subject half. "Helps or hurts" is informal. |
| 5 | **Cross-Subject Generalization and Chronological Personalization of Temperature and Humidity Estimation from Smart-Mat Pressure Sequences** | Neutral; covers both research questions and the input modality. | Does not signal the negative-transfer finding. Reads like a topic list. |
| 6 | **Chronological Personalization for Smart-Mat Temperature and Humidity Estimation under Unseen-User Domain Shift** | Clean and short (12 words). | Could read as "personalization works". It relies on the abstract for the caveat. |
| 7 | **Robust Temperature and Humidity Estimation from Smart-Mat Pressure Sequences under Cross-Subject Shift and Chronological Personalization** | Engineering tone. | "Robust" overstates the findings: several failure cases are central to the paper. **Not recommended.** |
| 8 | **Level Offsets, Temporal Drift and Few-Night Personalization in Smart-Mat Temperature and Humidity Estimation** | Mechanism-oriented vocabulary that matches the evidence. | Less searchable. "Few-night" is unusual in a title. |

- **Recommendation for PI review:** 1 or 2. Both carry the primary claim without overstatement. Rank 3 is the
  conservative fallback if a shorter, less specific title is preferred.
- **Avoid in any title:**
  - "robust" or "generalizable" as properties of the method;
  - "few-shot", which implies reliable success with 1–3 nights;
  - "first" or "novel";
  - anything that implies a population, e.g. "users" in general without qualification.
